### Pause 1. Summer exhibition

The [Royal Academy](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/) is one of the best galleries you can visit next time you’re in London, but among the great stuff they have all year around, the [Summer exhibition](https://www.royalacademy.org.uk/exhibition/summer-exhibition-2026) is by far my favourite. Once a year, pro and amateur artists, from every possible discipline, can submit their best work. The selection turns the rooms of this gallery into a proper art bazaar, where techniques and subjects get juxtaposed to great effect. Almost 2000 pieces of the most contemporary art possible—how is that for new ideas?

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!w2FA!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F33d7a4d1-db63-4793-bb34-99b110a1a0b7_3840x2160.png)

Don’t worry, nobody is trying to scam you. It is just a bunch of artists trying to make it. The new and original take over the walls with the price tags in a little booklet in your hands—from [40](https://se.royalacademy.org.uk/2026/artworks?page=1&priceRange=under-250) to [40,000](https://se.royalacademy.org.uk/2026/artworks?page=1&priceRange=10000-over); what is your budget? This is the time in the year where you can walk into one of the best galleries in the city and walk out owning one of the exhibited pieces.

And let me tell you something, we can theorise about the true value of art, and where it’s rooted and how crooked the galleries circuit is. When you are in front of a painting that you like so much to consider buying, the thought that most likely will pop in your head is: “Isn’t it a bit small for this money?”

### Pause 2. Odisea

Four months before our daughter was born we did our last holiday as mere adults in Mallorca. It wasn’t my first time on the island but it was the first time I connected with that arid and luscious nature next to a sky and sea that come together under the fuzzy sunshine. No wonder the Greeks ended up writing about all those gods and mythical creatures around this sea—I ended up thinking.

It must have been my midlife crisis that had me reading the [Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odyssey) at that time. Maybe it is just the human aesthetic response to an objectively beautiful place. Or maybe it is just that millennial arrogance that has me writing about this place as if I were the first one to experience this. No wonder, after almost two decades grinding it out in London, my uncles, Óscar & Matthew, decided to spend most of the year in Ibiza.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!dTUw!,w_2400,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F753b2582-ed1d-4929-9731-90b057dc37b2_5276x2164.png)

Here we are, guests for the second year in a row, no more noise than the farm waking up, next to a perfect pool for a quick dip, on a magical island to drive, with the book we saved for the holiday, and an LLM waiting for instructions that has started to knock on the screen.
