### Growth Pill. The circle of taste

Taste seems to be the new hot word that keeps popping up lately, but do we know what we are talking about?

It’s funny how in my uni years, when “Design” meant mostly “Graphic Design” we would insist to our friends and family that design was so much more than making things look nice. A bit later, we divided the roles. The UXers would be drawing flows and grey boxes so the UI or Visual Designers (I still remember my friend JuanRa wondering in class how could there be a design that is not visual, but that is a fight for another day) could make things look, if not nice, on brand. Now that LLMs seem to let anyone code anything we desperately look for our differentiator and the word that comes out is “taste”. Anybody can code anything but we’ll make it look nice.

While there is some truth to this, and I believe designers have a bit of an advantage in this brave new world (disclaimer, everyone thinks they are going to “win”, so nothing meaningful there), it is a mistake to think of it as some mysterious wizardry only accessible to a few chosen ones. [We have schools to learn design and we get better at it through years of practice, not by a secret handshake](https://x.com/karrisaarinen/status/2024157578943742192?s=20). I would say more: everybody designs, everyone has taste whether they make a living of it or not. (We can leave this controversy for a later issue).

### Live. Do we still need Figma?

One of the hopes when starting this newsletter was to share some of the process that has helped us to move to a new way of designing with the help of AI. This means that now you can also enjoy Design Sobremesa in Live format.

The key contribution here is the fact that we are not trying to use AI to rethink a whole website, platform or look and feel for us. Instead, we are trying to define a specific flow, component or information architecture, in a very controlled way like we would do in Figma. Producing a coded artefact instead of the sequence of static images (or the noodled prototype we used to do) means that we get to stay closer to its future implementation, plus we get to communicate and validate our ideas much more easily. Stay tuned.
