### Growth Pill 1. Designer’s super powers

My design professor, mentor and now great friend, [JuanRa Martín](https://juanramartin.es/), explains designers as people with a superpower: the power of making visible and tangible what others are barely imagining. Translating it to product-speak, designers are experts in the art of solutionizing. Our colleagues in the Product Team are the best at understanding a certain problem. They will pull against the urge of everyone in the team to jump to a specific solution to make sure we are not missing critical information or that we are not going down the wrong path misguided by our own taste. In contrast, the energy of a strong designer can drive the conversation forward working directly from the solution.

One of the ways of recognizing great design is that it will get everyone in the room excited. The developer can’t wait to code that; the sales person can’t wait to sell that; or the marketing guy is already happy about the post they’re going to write around that feature. There is nothing more exciting for a designer than finding the right idea, and when that happens we should move it forward with confidence, even if it contradicts everything I said in the previous paragraph. That’s why our teams are cross-functional, so we have different skillsets and information flowing in multiple directions so everyone gets the best of each other.

### Rambling 1. Meta-rambling

It is good to give credit where credit is due; I should probably get over with sharing where I borrowed this idea of the ramblings from. [Steph Ango](https://stephango.com/about), creator of one of my favorite apps, [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), posted a year ago [If you’re remote ramble](https://stephango.com/ramblings). A very actionable substitution of the watercooler talk. Each team member gets a public slack channel set up so only the author can post on it and everyone in the company can comment. Then we use those to share all that random stuff that we find interesting and never now in which channel to post. Liam Charnock and I have been doing this at [Viooh](https://www.viooh.com/) for a while already and we are loving it.

### Rambling 2. Ben Affleck

One week later, I must be the latest to the party sharing Ben Affleck’s thoughts on AI. Yet I’ll just share it again if only because he summarises perfectly my current thoughts on Generative AI.

-   It’s output is very average — he goes as far as saying shitty… that could be its own rambling)
    
-   It’s a useful TOOL if you are a writer — I would add any other creative job. Capitals are mine
    
-   I don’t think it’s going to be able to write anything meaningful
    
-   Laws to protect your likeness – and IP – already exist.
    
-   A lot of the rhetoric around existential threat comes from people that have to justify valuations.
    
-   The scaling of AI – or its economics, Gabriel Castor– seems to be plateauing
    

Only time will tell how right my buddy Ben and I are.
