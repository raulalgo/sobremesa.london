In Spain we say “New year, new life” but I could very well be talking about my new year’s resolutions. This is why I’m planning to come here weekly write and ramble about everything that sparks my curiosity or catches my attention in the world of Digital Product Design

# One last balance

You thought you were done with 2025 wrap ups? I didn’t do mine last year so you’ll have to suffer through this. Luckily it is also a perfect set up for the new year and what I’m trying to capture here.

2025 was the year when our team at [Viooh](https://www.viooh.com/) got serious about adding AI to our design and discovery process. Ignacio Rovira(who I hope eventually drops by this space too) with the learnings from [Programa Vostok](https://tramontana.net/vostok/) introduced us all to the field. Since then we onboarded a team of 4 designers first and then well over 15 PMs to [V0](http://v0.app/). It worked so well for us that we even got the design team to join [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) and get prototyping directly in the local environment of our codebase. It has been a year of a lot of trial and error, in which we have produced our first prototypes to ideate and validate in a way that would have been impossible just by drawing mockups or prototyping in Figma.

2026 will be the year we consolidate and scale **vibe coding as a design tool**.By getting our work closer to code we can inform our decision making not only of user-centricity but of technical feasibility too. And we can do that by producing artifacts that are shareable and fully interactive, simplifying communication across multi-disciplinary teams and tightening the discovery loop with faster and better user validation.

This connects with the other big theme in my 2025. In spring I attended my first [BEDA](https://beda.org/) forum and General assembly in Brussels. It was a great experience to get to know first hand the people that are trying to make design in Europe a force to drive positivef change and innovation. For the new year, I’ve been lucky enough to be selected to take part in their ambitious project [MADres](https://madres.beda.org/) as part of the AI & Digital Ethics pillar of their [G42 panel of experts](https://madres.beda.org/en/glorious-42/). Expect more AI, more Europe and, above all, more Design coming this way.
